# Literature-to-Module Matrix

This matrix records research directions motivating candidate components. It does not establish that their combination is effective for SIH-26038. “TODO” means the bibliographic detail, applicability, or implementation choice must be verified before use.

## Verified Dataset Evidence

| Dataset/source | Verified fact | Architecture relevance | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [APTOS 2019 official competition data](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/APTOS2019-blindness-detection/data) | Clinician-rated DR grades 0–4; training CSV/images and test data; real-world quality variation across clinics, cameras, and time. | Primary five-class grading and motivation for quality-aware robustness research. | Do not assume public competition test labels or patient metadata. Quality variation is not an official binary quality label; apply competition terms. |
| [IDRiD Grand Challenge data](https://idrid.grand-challenge.org/Data/) and [data descriptor](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/3/3/25) | 516 JPG images, 0–4 DR grades, official 413/103 grading split; lesions for 81 images only; OD/fovea centers for 516. | Supports grading plus partial lesion/anatomical supervision and lesion-grounded evaluation. | Lesion labels are incomplete and unequal (MA 81, EX 81, HE 80, SE 40); do not assume every grade image has a mask. |
| [DRIVE official Grand Challenge](https://drive.grand-challenge.org/) | 40 JPEG images; 20 training images have manual vessel masks; FOV masks available; test vessel ground truth is not public. | Candidate auxiliary vessel/anatomical representation research. | Not a general DR grading dataset; do not evaluate its test set without legitimately obtained official ground truth. |
| [Messidor-2 official ADCIS source](https://www.adcis.net/en/third-party/messidor2/) | 874 examinations / 1748 paired macula-centered images; Original PNG and Extension JPG; official source provides pairing information. | Candidate future external/domain-shift data. | **Official release has no DR ground truth.** Verify third-party labels for provenance, license, semantics, and scientific validity before any external DR evaluation. |

| Evidence-reliability gate | Quality and prediction uncertainty are associated with trustworthiness concerns in DR grading. | [DR|GRADUATE](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2020.101715); [DeepDRiD](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214346/) | Combine reportability signals rather than relying on grade confidence alone. | Helps prevent overconfident recommendations from poor-quality or uncertain images. | Pre-specify and validate gate rules; measure unsafe-report rate and unnecessary retakes. | Research hypothesis; policy TODO. |
